# Project Purpose and Goal

QuickHost is a leading provider of **web hosting and domain registration services in Kenya**. The project focused on **redesigning the website to enhance customer experience, optimize performance, and reinforce brand consistency**. The previous site had an outdated structure, making navigation difficult for users. The goal was to create a **modern, intuitive platform** that simplifies the hosting process for both beginners and professionals while ensuring **faster performance and better engagement**.

# Problems and Thought Process

One of the main challenges was ensuring that **QuickHost’s services were clearly communicated and accessible** to a diverse audience.

- **For beginners**, we needed a straightforward, user-friendly flow that made domain registration and web hosting selection easier.
- **For professionals**, we built a **dedicated section** with advanced hosting options like **VPS and dedicated servers** to cater to their technical needs.

### **Performance Optimization**

The original website suffered from **slow load times** due to **unoptimized scripts and large assets**. To fix this, I:

- **Optimized images** and implemented **lazy loading** to reduce page weight.
- **Removed unnecessary scripts** that were slowing down performance.
- **Refined caching strategies** to improve **page load speeds**.

### **SEO & Google Ads Integration**

To increase organic traffic and improve lead generation, I worked on:

- Implementing **SEO best practices** to improve search engine visibility.
- Running **targeted Google Ads campaigns** for hosting and domain-related services.
- Improving **structured metadata and content hierarchy** for better search ranking.

# Web Stack and Explanation

QuickHost’s website was built using **WordPress**, leveraging its flexibility for content management.

- **TailwindCSS** was used for modern styling and responsiveness.
- **Figma** was utilized for the redesign and iterative improvements.
- **Google Analytics** was integrated to track **user behavior and performance insights**.

# Lessons Learned & Future Work

The **redesign significantly improved customer engagement**, making it easier for users to explore services and complete purchases. Moving forward, the plan is to:

- Further **enhance SEO strategies** for sustained growth.
- Implement **A/B testing** for UI/UX optimizations based on user interactions.
- Introduce **automated chatbot support** for improved customer service.
