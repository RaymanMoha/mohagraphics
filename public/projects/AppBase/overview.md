# Product Overview

AppBase is a personal commerce product designed for Kenyan businesses that already sell through WhatsApp. It keeps the customer journey inside the conversation while giving merchants a structured system for catalogues, carts, M-Pesa payment requests, verified payment callbacks, order status, and branded receipts.

## User Need

Small merchants often manage product questions, payment screenshots, order details, and fulfilment manually across chat and spreadsheets. They needed a simpler sales loop that felt natural to customers while giving the business reliable proof of payment and a clear record of every order.

## Engineering Focus

- Designed the end-to-end path from product question to combined cart, M-Pesa request, verified payment, fulfilment, and receipt.
- Built a responsive product website and merchant-facing story around real Kenyan commerce habits.
- Structured approved, product-aware assistance so automation supports the merchant without hiding control.
- Connected catalogue, payment, customer, and order states into one accountable commerce workflow.

## Role

Product Engineer · Personal Product

## Outcome

- Produced a working, pilot-ready product proposition for WhatsApp-first Kenyan merchants.
- Made payment verification and order accountability the centre of the experience instead of relying on screenshots or informal confirmation.
- Created a clear commercial path from guided setup to a repeatable merchant subscription.
