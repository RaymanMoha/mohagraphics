# Budj

## Project Overview

Budj is a merchant suite with a guided registration experience for new businesses. The merchant onboarding page at `merchant.budj.app` walks users through a three-step flow, starting with Business Info and capturing core details before advancing to the next stage.

## Registration Experience

- Headline and value proposition: "Join Thousands of Merchants" and a short promise about transforming how businesses manage finances.
- Clear onboarding intent: "Become a Budj Merchant" with "Complete your registration in a few simple steps".
- Step indicator: "Business Info" shown as Step 1/3 to set expectations.
- Core fields: Business Name, Business Category (combobox), and Business Description.
- Primary CTA: a single "Next" button to advance the flow.

## UI and Trust Signals

- Split layout with a bold brand panel on the left and a clean form card on the right.
- Merchant Suite badge, logo lockup, and animated assistant illustration for brand reinforcement.
- Security reassurance: "Bank-grade security & encryption".
- Supporting links: sign-in, privacy policy, terms of service, and support.
