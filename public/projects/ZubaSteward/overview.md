# Product Overview

Zuba Steward supports gate staff verifying tickets for Botswana Premier League matches. It complements the Zuba fan app within the Reon Capital portfolio.

## User Need

Matchday teams need to confirm admission quickly while keeping track of activity at their gates.

## Product Experience

- Scan digital ticket QR codes with the camera.
- Check both single-match tickets and season passes.
- Monitor gate-entry activity in real time.

## Outcome

Published on Google Play as ZUBA - Steward, with its own listing and screenshots.
