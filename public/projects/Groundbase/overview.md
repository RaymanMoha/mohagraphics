# Product Overview

Groundbase is a personal product concept for teams whose work happens away from a desk. It combines no-code operational forms, offline field capture, GPS and photo evidence, dashboards, and AI-generated insights so managers can move from scattered updates to a reliable view of what is happening on the ground.

## User Need

Field teams in construction, agriculture, logistics, property, and other operational industries often rely on paper, WhatsApp, and spreadsheets. Leaders needed a clearer way to collect trusted evidence, spot issues early, and turn daily activity into decisions without adding a heavy enterprise system.

## Engineering Focus

- Designed an AI-assisted form-building journey that starts with plain-language process descriptions.
- Shaped offline-ready capture around GPS, photos, timestamps, signatures, logic, and calculations.
- Organised AI insights, reports, visual analysis, and predictive alerts into an understandable product system.
- Built a responsive product narrative for both field users and operations decision-makers.

## Role

## Outcome

- Turned a broad operations idea into a coherent product with a clear audience, workflow, and value proposition.
- Demonstrated how AI can reduce reporting work while preserving traceable field evidence.
- Created a flexible foundation for industry-specific use cases across construction, agriculture, logistics, and property operations.
