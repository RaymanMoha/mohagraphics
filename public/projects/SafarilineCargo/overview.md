## Product Overview

Cargo moves through many hands. The interface needs to keep the whole journey understandable.

Safariline Cargo brings air and sea operations into one web workspace: waybills, manifests, warehouse facilities, tracking, customer and supplier accounts, approvals, and reports. Its dashboard introduces the product with a clear promise: **Movement made visible.**

## User Need

Operations teams need to answer practical questions quickly: where is the cargo, which branch holds it, what stage has it reached, and which records need attention? A shared navigation system connects those questions to the relevant shipment, facility, or account.

## Product Experience

- **Start with the network.** A branch-aware overview combines shipment counts, freight modes, recent activity, and shortcuts to tracking and reporting. All-branch access is explicitly presented as read-only.
- **Find the waybill.** The shipment register brings route, volume, manifest, and status together, with filters for origin, destination, freight mode, and shipment status.
- **Follow physical custody.** Warehouse facilities distinguish origin staging, in-transit locations, and destination custody, alongside air and sea stock volumes.
- **Connect operations to accounts.** Customer and supplier sections sit alongside reporting for statements, payments, route revenue, shipment status, and warehouse dwell.

## Outcome

A connected operations experience that gives shipment movement, warehouse activity, and financial records a shared home. The case study follows that progression through screenshots of the working development application.
