# Product Overview

OnSpace is a no-code operations platform for building internal business applications without writing custom software for every workflow. Teams use it to create forms, collect field data, manage submissions, collaborate around records, and turn daily operations into searchable, structured workflows across web and mobile.

## User Need

Many operations teams still run daily reporting, field work, stock requisitions, sales tracking, lead management, and feedback collection through spreadsheets, WhatsApp messages, and disconnected tools. OnSpace needed to make those workflows feel simple enough for non-technical teams while still giving managers clean records, member visibility, and reporting structure.

## Product Experience

- Workspace and folders experience for managing multiple internal forms.
- Searchable form list for Field Work Tracker, Daily Sales Reporting, Stock Requisitions, Leads Management, and Customer Feedback.
- Chat-style submission feed where each record keeps form fields, timestamps, users, linked emails, and location data readable.
- Mobile app experience for teams that need to access folders, forms, and submissions from the field.
- Web dashboard patterns for admins who need larger-screen review, search, analytics, settings, templates, and team management.
- Blog/resources experience for explaining no-code operations, business functions, internal applications, templates, and use cases.
