# Product Overview

Sava is a fuel commerce and wallet mobile app project completed under Reon Capital, alongside products like ENEVA, Zuba, and ANSU. The app connects customers, fuel stations, delivery trucks, and business owners through a clean mobile-first experience for prepaid fuel, order placement, location discovery, and operational tracking.

## User Need

Fuel customers needed a faster way to preload funds, find nearby stations, order fuel, and track delivery details. Business owners needed visibility into wallet balances, order status, withdrawals, and recent transactions without relying on manual follow-ups.

## Product Experience

- Customer wallet with balance display, add-funds entry points, and quick actions.
- Preload money flow with fixed amounts, custom amount input, and payment method selection.
- Fuel station discovery screen with search, filters, map state, fuel types, hours, and navigation actions.
- Owner dashboard showing current balance, withdrawals, wallet access, order overview, and transaction history.
- Truck information view with license plate, fuel type, delivery capacity, shipping code, and map context.
