# Product Overview

Yala Pay is a fintech platform serving businesses in East Africa. The marketing site aligns with the Flutter app branding and supports user acquisition.

## User Need

Businesses needed a clear overview of the payment platform and a direct path to contact and onboarding.

## Product Experience

- Landing pages aligned to the mobile app UI.
- Content structure for product, features, and contact.
- SEO setup and Google Ads landing support.

## Role

Frontend Developer

## Outcome

- Delivered a consistent brand experience between web and mobile.

# Tech Stack

- WordPress
- SEO, Google Ads
- Flutter app branding alignment
