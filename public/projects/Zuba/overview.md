# Zuba: Botswana Premier League Digital Platform

## Project Overview

Zuba represents a groundbreaking digital transformation initiative for the Botswana Premier League (BPL), creating a comprehensive ecosystem that connects fans, stewards, and administrators through cutting-edge mobile and web applications. This platform revolutionizes how football is experienced and managed in Botswana.

## Key Applications

### 📱 **Zuba Fan App**
The primary fan engagement platform designed to bring supporters closer to their favorite teams and the league.

**Core Features:**
- **Live Match Updates**: Real-time scores, statistics, and commentary
- **Team Profiles**: Comprehensive information about all BPL teams
- **Player Statistics**: Detailed player profiles and performance metrics
- **Match Schedules**: Complete fixture lists and venue information
- **News & Updates**: Latest BPL news and announcements
- **Fan Community**: Social features for supporter interaction
- **Push Notifications**: Instant alerts for goals, match starts, and breaking news
- **AI-Powered Analytics**: Advanced player and team performance insights


### 👮 **Zuba Steward App**
Professional management tool for match officials and stewards.

**Administrative Features:**
- **Match Management**: Digital match sheets and reporting
- **Incident Reporting**: Real-time incident documentation
- **Communication Hub**: Direct communication with league officials
- **Duty Scheduling**: Steward assignment and scheduling system
- **Training Modules**: Educational content and certification tracking
- **Emergency Protocols**: Quick access to safety procedures
