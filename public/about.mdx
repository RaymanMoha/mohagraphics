### My quick story

Based in Nairobi, Kenya, I'm open to remote and Kenya-based frontend and mobile roles.

My journey in tech started with a deep passion for digital creation. I began by focusing on WordPress, HTML, and CSS to build engaging websites that captured my imagination and allowed me to experiment with design and functionality. I spent hours learning the ins and outs of crafting online experiences, and those early projects laid the groundwork for my understanding of digital aesthetics and user engagement.

Later, my pursuit of growth led me to ALX, where I completed an intensive full-stack curriculum. This immersive experience not only honed my technical skills but also provided a comprehensive view of software engineering—from backend systems to modern frontend frameworks. It was during this period that I discovered my love for frontend development, drawn by the creative and user-centric aspects of UI/UX design. I found that I thrived on the challenge of transforming intricate designs into intuitive, pixel-perfect interfaces that truly resonate with users.

Today, I combine the foundational skills from my early experiments with the advanced techniques I learned at ALX to build digital experiences that are both innovative and accessible. Each project is a new opportunity to push boundaries, learn something new, and create interfaces that are not just functional, but also delight users.


### As a developer

![Tech GIF](/img/pablita-516.gif)

**My coding ethos is centered on clarity and accessibility.** Initially, I build to ensure functionality, but I dedicate considerable effort to refining my code - commenting, organizing, and ensuring it's welcoming to any collaborator. _Karibu_ in the world of code.

**Efficiency and maintainability are my mantras.** Functional features are good, but sustainable, and adaptable code is the gold standard. It’s not just about making it work; it’s about crafting it to last and be easily managed.

I thrive on new projects, as each one is a new chapter of learning and growth. **Every challenge is an opportunity to expand my horizons.**
